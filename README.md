# 💡 Why Alzheimer Lens?

Alzheimer's disease can affect memory, recognition, and a person's ability to navigate familiar everyday situations.

For someone experiencing memory loss, a familiar face may not always feel familiar.

A simple moment can become a question:

> **"Who is this person?"**

And everyday routines can raise other questions:

> **"What am I supposed to do next?"**

> **"Where am I?"**

> **"Who can I contact if I need help?"**

These questions inspired **Alzheimer Lens**.

Instead of creating another system that requires the user to constantly interact with a phone or screen, we explored a wearable approach:

> **What if useful information could come to the user naturally, at the moment it is needed?**

Alzheimer Lens combines:

**👓 Wearable Technology**  
**🧠 Artificial Intelligence**  
**📷 Computer Vision**  
**🔊 Voice Assistance**  
**☁️ Cloud Connectivity**  
**🌐 Companion Software**

into one connected prototype.

The goal is **not** to replace caregivers, doctors, or human interaction.

The goal is to explore how technology can provide an additional layer of assistance — helping everyday moments feel a little more familiar, manageable, and connected.

# 👓 What Is Alzheimer Lens?

Alzheimer Lens is a **Raspberry Pi-based smart glasses prototype** designed to assist people with Alzheimer’s in everyday situations.

The system combines a camera, AI-based facial recognition, voice assistance, a Micro OLED display, GPS, connectivity, and an SOS interface.

When a registered familiar person appears in front of the user:

```text
👤 Familiar Person
        ↓
📷 Camera
        ↓
🧠 AI Recognition
        ↓
👤 Person Identified
        ↓
🔊 Voice + 🖥️ OLED
